Apricus — a Free MEE6 Alternative for Discord, Without the Leveling Paywall

Apricus is a free Discord leveling bot: /rank, /leaderboard, /xpstats and /levelup announcements are all included, with nothing locked behind a premium tier. It sets itself up in about 30 seconds with /quicksetup.

Add Apricus to your server

Updated 2026-11-02. Every command named on this page is live in the bot's command tree today.

If you searched for a “MEE6 alternative free” or asked on Reddit which leveling bot is not paywalled, the short answer is: pick a bot where the leveling commands are not the paid product. Apricus is one project, one binary, one .env file, run by people who got tired of enabling a level system and then being told level roles cost money. It is not a clone and it does not claim feature parity with MEE6 — MEE6 has years of polish and a much bigger feature surface. What Apricus does is keep leveling, moderation, welcome messages, starboard, suggestions and polls free, and let you host it yourself if you want to.

Leveling that stays free

/rank shows a member's level and XP, /leaderboard ranks the server, /globalboard ranks across servers, and /xpstats breaks down where XP comes from. /levelup posts the level-up message. No tier gate on any of them.

30-second setup

/quicksetup configures leveling, welcome and goodbye messages in one pass. /setup and /setup_status cover the details, and /tour walks you through what the bot can actually do in your server.

Welcome & roles

/welcome set and /goodbye set handle join/leave messages, /autorole set hands out roles on join, and /onboard covers the first-minute experience for new members.

Community tools

/starboard set surfaces good messages, /suggest plus /suggestions run a suggestion board, and /newpoll runs a poll with a live results channel.

Moderation included

/ban, /kick, /mute, /purge, /warn and /warnings are ordinary parts of the bot, not an upsell. /serverinfo and /botinfo give you the state at a glance. /addbot - get a clean link to add Apricus to another server.

Utilities you will use

/remind and /reminders for scheduled pings, /daily for a daily claim, /feedback to report issues straight to the developer, and /help or /usage when you forget a name.

What “free” means here, honestly

  • There are no premium tiers, no subscription and no “unlock level roles” screen — because there is no billing code in the project at all.
  • There is no AI chat feature. Apricus answers with commands and data, not with a language model in your chat.
  • We do not publish server counts or member counts we cannot verify. The honest number is: it runs on the servers you add it to.
  • It is not a MEE6 clone. If you need MEE6's specific dashboard, stream alerts or its plugin marketplace, Apricus is not that product.

What you get, and what you do not

Included, free

  • Leveling: /rank, /leaderboard, /globalboard, /xpstats, /levelup, /addxp
  • Onboarding: /welcome set, /goodbye set, /autorole set, /onboard
  • Community: /starboard set, /suggest, /suggestions, /newpoll, /poll
  • Moderation and utility: /warn, /warnings, /purge, /remind, /reminders, /daily

Not included

  • No AI chat replies in your channels.
  • No premium store, no subscription tiers, nothing to cancel.
  • No hosted web dashboard; configuration happens through commands.
  • No import of XP history from another bot's database.

Is there a free MEE6 alternative that is actually free?

Yes, but read the fine print on any candidate before you migrate. A leveling bot is “free” in a useful sense only if the commands your members touch every day — rank cards, the leaderboard, level-up announcements, XP roles — are enabled without a payment step. Apricus keeps all of those free. The trade-off is that Apricus is a smaller project: fewer integrations, no hosted dashboard, and a command list you should read rather than assume. Run /help after inviting and it will show exactly what exists in your server.

MEE6 alternative, according to Reddit — what should I actually compare?

Ignore the vote counts and compare four things: (1) which leveling commands are behind a paywall on the incumbent, (2) whether the alternative stores your data and where, (3) how long setup takes when you are not reading a wiki, and (4) what happens to your server if you stop paying. Apricus answers (2) with “a hosted service with a small, documented data footprint”, (3) with /quicksetup in roughly 30 seconds, and (4) with “nothing, there is nothing to stop paying for”. For (1), check the current pricing page of whatever you are leaving — it changes.

Can I move from MEE6 to Apricus without losing my server?

You will not lose your server, but XP history does not transfer: levels start from zero because Apricus keeps its own XP database and cannot read another bot's storage. The safe path is to invite Apricus, run /quicksetup, run /tour to see the feature list, keep the old bot's leveling muted while members adjust, and use /addxp to credit long-time members so nobody starts from nothing. Use /setup_status to confirm what is enabled before you announce the switch.

Can I earn Apricots for adding Apricus to a new server?

Yes. /installref gives you a personal install link and /installboard shows the referral leaderboard, so you earn Apricots when Apricus lands in a NEW server through your link. Servers that already have Apricus do not count a second time.
